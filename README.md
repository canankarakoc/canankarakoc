## Canan Karakoç

Evolutionary ecologist working with microbes. I combine experiments, data synthesis and eco-evolutionary theory to understand, and ultimately predict, how populations and communities persist and adapt as their environment changes. Research scholar in the [Brown Lab](https://brownlab.biology.gatech.edu/) at Georgia Tech, supported by a Cystic Fibrosis Foundation Postdoctoral Fellowship.

**Research themes**

- **Energy:** what it costs cells to persist, from the ATP budget of making a spore to evolution during 1,000 days of starvation.
- **Predictability:** when infection outcomes can be forecast, from host health and survival to the [accelerating risk of death](https://doi.org/10.1128/msphere.00953-24) across host-pathogen systems.
- **Noise:** how environmental fluctuations and sampling noise shape host-phage dynamics and what routine resistance testing can reveal.
